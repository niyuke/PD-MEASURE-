Measure your pupillary distance

Your PD is the distance between your pupils. It centres the lenses in your glasses.

  1. Take off your glasses and face a window or lamp.
  2. Hold your phone, or sit, so your face is about 40 cm from the camera.
  3. Look at the blue dot just under the camera and hold still. It measures on its own.
