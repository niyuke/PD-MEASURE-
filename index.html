Measure your pupillary distance

Your PD is the distance between your pupils. It centres the lenses in your glasses.

  1. Take off your glasses and face a window or lamp.
  2. Hold a bank card flat against your forehead, long side level, above your eyebrows. Pinch it at the middle of its left and right sides so it stays straight.
  3. Keep your face about 40 cm from the camera, look at the blue dot and hold still. It measures on its own.

Open on its own page Any bank card works. It is found and photographed automatically.

Mark the card

The card was found automatically. The magnifier (8×) shows the selected corner. Drag a dot onto the card's corner, or use the arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps) or the buttons for tiny moves. If it was not found, tap the four corners.

Fine-tune by hand

  1. Move the yellow circle so the iris sits inside it, centred on the pupil. Drag inside the circle, or use the arrows for tiny moves. The circle has a fixed size, set by the scale.
  2. The small green cross marks the circle's centre. Place it exactly on the middle of the pupil.
  3. Drag the white line to the middle of your nose bridge.
